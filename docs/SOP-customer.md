# How to Use BookLingua
*A step-by-step guide for authors*

---

## What is BookLingua?

BookLingua translates your book into up to 6 languages using a two-pass AI system — a full translation followed by an editorial review that refines tone, phrasing, and cultural accuracy. Every translation includes highlighted changes so you can see exactly what was refined, plus a set of Translation Notes explaining key decisions.

**Website:** https://booklingua.io

---

## Step 1 — Upload Your Book

Click **"Upload Your Book"** on the homepage.

**Supported formats:**
- EPUB *(recommended — best for formatting preservation)*
- DOCX
- PDF
- TXT

**File size limit:** 50MB

Your formatting — chapter headings, paragraph breaks, italics — is preserved in the output.

---

## Step 2 — Book Details

Fill in a few details so the translation is tailored to your book:

- **Title** — your book's title
- **Genre** — helps the AI match the right style and vocabulary (e.g. Romance, Thriller, Fantasy)
- **Heat Level** *(Romance/Erotica only)* — choose from Sweet, Steamy, or Explicit so intimate scenes are translated accurately
- **Book Setting** *(optional)* — describe the time period, world, or cultural context if relevant
- **Special Instructions** *(optional)* — any specific requests, e.g. "keep character names in English" or "use formal register throughout"

## After Payment — What Happens Next

Once your payment is confirmed, here's the timeline:

| Step | What's happening | Time |
|------|-----------------|------|
| 1 | Translation Pass — AI translates your full manuscript, preserving your voice and formatting | ~1–2 hours |
| 2 | Editorial Review — A second AI pass refines tone, cultural accuracy, and natural phrasing. Changes are highlighted in yellow. | ~1 hour |
| 3 | Delivery — Your translated file and Translation Notes are emailed to you | Within minutes of completion |

**Total time: typically 2–3 hours.**

You'll receive two emails:
1. **Order confirmation** — sent immediately after payment
2. **Your translation** — sent when the files are ready, with download links

## Your Translated Files

You'll receive:
- **Translated manuscript** — in the same format you uploaded (EPUB → EPUB, DOCX → DOCX, etc.)
- **Translation Notes** — a document explaining the key editorial decisions and cultural adaptations made during the review

Changes made in the editorial pass are **highlighted in yellow**, so you can review every refinement.

Download links are time-limited. Save your files as soon as you receive them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this better than Google Translate?**
Yes — significantly. BookLingua uses a two-pass process: translation first, then editorial review that adapts phrasing, cultural references, and tone. Every change is visible so you stay in control.

**What file formats do you support?**
EPUB, PDF, DOCX, and TXT. DOCX gives the best formatting results.

**How long does translation take?**
Most books complete in 2–3 hours. You'll get an email with your download link when it's ready.

**Can I see an example first?**
Yes — visit [booklingua.io/examples](https://booklingua.io/examples) to see a real translation with editorial highlights and Translation Notes.

**What if I'm not happy with the translation?**
Email us within 7 days at support@booklingua.io and we'll work with you to make it right.

**Do you offer pricing for publishers with multiple books?**
Yes — visit [booklingua.io/publishers](https://booklingua.io/publishers) or email hello@booklingua.io for custom bulk pricing.
