# How to Prepare Your Manuscript for Translation

Getting your book translated starts long before you click "upload." A clean, well-structured manuscript makes the difference between a translation that needs heavy editing and one that's ready to publish.

## 1. Choose the Right File Format

BookLingua accepts **EPUB, DOCX, and TXT**. For the best results, we recommend DOCX:

- Headings and styles are preserved
- Chapter structure is detected automatically
- Word counts are most accurate

EPUB works well too, but complex layouts can shift during conversion. TXT is fine for simple manuscripts but loses all formatting.

## 2. Clean Up Your Styles

Use **Heading 1** for chapter titles and **Heading 2** for sub-sections. Avoid manual formatting (large bold text, extra line breaks) where possible. A cleanly styled DOCX translates into a cleanly structured book.

## 3. List Cultural References and Special Terms

If your book includes invented words, place names, or culturally specific terms, tell us upfront. During the pre-translation scan, BookLingua flags terms like:

- US-specific legal or education terms
- Measurements (miles, pounds, Fahrenheit)
- Brand names and idioms
- Fantasy names and invented language

You decide whether to keep, adapt, or explain each one.

## 4. Write a Short Book Setting Note

A sentence or two about where the book is set and what local flavour should be preserved helps the translator make better decisions. For example:

> "Set in New York. Keep legal terms American and convert measurements to metric for European readers."

## 5. Check Your Word Count

Pricing is based on word count. Most novellas fall into the $99 tier, standard novels into the $149 tier, and longer books into the $199 tier. The uploader shows your exact price before checkout.
